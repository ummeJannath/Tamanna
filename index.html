How I got here

Growing up in Bangladesh, my design journey started with drawing As a teen, art and graffiti sparked my passion to visual storytelling even more. Later, an Software Engineering degree gave me foundation, and digital design showed new creative depths and that's when I realized this is where I want to have the most fun of my life.

These days, my husband and I call Dhaka, Bangladesh, home. I spend my days working with talented people across different teams, turning ideas and challenges into thoughtful digital experiences. Outside of my full-time work, I occasionally take on independent projects that let me explore new ideas, experiment with creativity, and create designs that speak louder than words.

Where I Stand

For over 5 years, I've been working with teams and businesses to turn ideas into meaningful digital experiences. From fintech and education to enterprise platforms, SaaS, and web applications, I've had the opportunity to work on products that solve real problems for real people.

I believe great design is more than just how something looks. It's about understanding people, making complex things feel simple, and creating experiences that build trust. My goal is to bring together thoughtful UX, purposeful visuals, and technology to make digital products more useful, accessible, and enjoyable.
