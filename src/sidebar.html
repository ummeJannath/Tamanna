My work is driven by a strong commitment to creating meaningful, user-centered digital experiences where form and function work seamlessly together. As a UI/UX Designer at BRAC IT Ltd, I work across UI/UX design, user research, prototyping, and design systems, with a focus on transforming complex requirements and workflows into intuitive, accessible, and visually refined products. My background in Software Engineering gives me a strong understanding of how products are built, allowing me to collaborate effectively with developers and bridge the gap between design and technology.

I hold a B.Sc. in Software Engineering from Daffodil International University and bring 5+ years of experience working across digital products and platforms.

My day-to-day toolkit includes Figma, Adobe XD, Photoshop, Illustrator, design systems, usability testing, HTML, and CSS, combining design thinking with technical understanding to ensure that every design decision has a clear purpose and contributes to a better user experience.
